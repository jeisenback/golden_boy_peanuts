## Scenario

**Real anchor event:** the 2021 Suez Canal obstruction — the container ship *Ever
Given* ran aground on 2021-03-23 07:40 UTC and blocked the canal in both directions
until it was freed on 2021-03-29 13:05 UTC (six days). This is a well-documented
tanker chokepoint disruption with a reported positive correlation to crude oil
price returns during the blockage week.
([Wikipedia: 2021 Suez Canal obstruction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Suez_Canal_obstruction))

**In-scope instrument:** `BZ=F` (Brent crude futures) — directly affected, since the
Suez Canal is a primary Europe/Asia crude and refined-products corridor.

**What's real vs. synthetic:** the event, its dates, and its documented oil-price
correlation are real and cited above. The specific shipping and narrative *records*
injected into the pipeline below are synthetic test data — no live MarineTraffic
or Reddit history was queried — dated to, and order-of-magnitude representative
of, the real event (the actual blockage produced hundreds of queued vessels and
extensive global media/social coverage). This mirrors the existing Phase 2 UAT
precedent (`112-uat-report-2026-03-19.md`), which also anchors an injected
`DetectedEvent` to a real historical date (the 2022-02-24 Ukraine invasion) rather
than replaying live data.
